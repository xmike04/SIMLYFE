# SIMLYFE Product Direction

SIMLYFE is a life strategy game: planning, trade-offs, and lasting consequences lead the experience. AI makes each life personal. This priority was confirmed by the project owner on October 15, 2026.

## Player promise

Build a life through decisions whose consequences shape your opportunities, obligations, relationships, and future choices. AI makes those consequences personal and responsive to the life you have actually lived.

Players should have enough information to plan around meaningful requirements and costs while encountering deliberate uncertainty. Important consequences belong in saved gameplay state. Different pursuits should offer worthwhile choices; accumulating wealth is one possible life strategy.

## Role of AI

The engine owns eligibility, costs, canonical entities, and committed outcomes. AI supplies contextual creative content within validated boundaries. Narration must reflect what happened; prose alone cannot establish a job, purchase, relationship, or payment. The existing annual interactive event remains a distinct, validated player-choice flow.

The career foundation exposes bounded factual results for future personalization. It does not yet add AI career narration or establish long-term generated-story memory.

## Feature decisions

Evaluate proposed mechanics by the decisions and future consequences they create, the clarity of their trade-offs, and how they connect to a particular life. Preserve mature themes, including crime, violence, adult relationships, and drugs. Voice and setting can evolve through explicit product decisions.

BitLife research informs general engineering questions about action resolution, persistence, and transient caches. SIMLYFE chooses its own mechanics, progression, presentation, and content. Interview quizzes, offer flows, or reference-game menus are not requirements.
