SIMLYFE privacy policy

Release draft prepared September 30, 2026.

Publisher identity, a monitored support contact, and operational retention details still require owner confirmation. This draft has not been approved for public distribution.

What SIMLYFE does

SIMLYFE is a fictional life simulation with cloud saves and AI-generated narrative events. Game money, character health, relationships, jobs and locations describe simulated characters, not verified financial, health or location information about you. Avoid entering sensitive real-world information as character names or other game text.

Identity and cloud saves

Firebase Authentication creates a guest identity. If you choose Google or email/password sign-in, Firebase processes those account credentials. The interface may display your account name, email and Google profile image. Your cloud life is stored in Firestore under that identity. Account credentials are not part of the saved game or AI prompt.

AI events

The app sends a bounded fictional-game snapshot, including character information, recent history, simulated stats and action context, to a Supabase Edge Function. It verifies a short-lived Firebase token, enforces quotas and sends game context to OpenAI to generate an event. The token, email account details and password are not included in the OpenAI prompt.

Reports and deletion requests

If you report an event, its description, choice text, reason and public generation request ID are stored in an owner-scoped support inbox with a server timestamp. Your full life and credentials are excluded. An account-deletion request stores its type and timestamp under your account identity. These requests are for developer review and do not immediately delete an account.

Storage and retention

Your current cloud life persists until replaced by a new life or removed through account deletion. Android keeps Firebase session and Firestore cache data locally, including pending writes. Clearing app data removes that local cache and may make an unlinked guest account unrecoverable.

The quota service stores a keyed hash of your Firebase identity, counters and timestamps. It prunes records older than seven days during a daily pruning pass triggered by subsequent generation traffic; this is not an exact seven-day deletion guarantee. Service providers may separately retain operational, security and backup records under their own policies. Support-request retention and deletion-response timing must be confirmed before release.

Deletion

Use Account → Request account deletion in the app, or the SIMLYFE web deletion page. On the web, sign in to the account you want deleted; the Android app is not required. A guest identity that is no longer accessible cannot be recovered by its fictional character name alone.

Requested deletion covers the Firebase account, current cloud life and associated support-request contents. Limited security, provider and backup records may remain subject to their retention policies. Signing out or starting a new life alone does not delete an account.

Permissions and advertising

The current Android build uses network access. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or storage access, and includes no advertising or billing SDK. Game purchases and simulated gambling use fictional in-game money.

Contact and changes

A public, monitored support contact must be added here before store distribution. Future material data-flow changes require updating this policy and the Play data-safety disclosures.
